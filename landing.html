How it works

From signup to first knock in three steps

Linkway is designed to get out of your way. There's nothing to install and no technical knowledge required.

1

Create your campaign

Sign up with a Google account. Linkway copies a campaign spreadsheet into your Drive and sets up your unique volunteer sign-in link.

2

Add your volunteers

Paste your volunteers' Google email addresses into the Approved Volunteers tab of your spreadsheet. That's all — they can sign in immediately.

3

Send them the canvassing link

Share your campaign's canvassing URL. Volunteers open it on their phone, sign in with Google, and start logging doors. Results appear in your Sheet in real time.
