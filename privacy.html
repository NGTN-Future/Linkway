Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Linkway ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and handles information when you use our canvassing and volunteer coordination service. We've written it to be plain and specific, not generic boilerplate.

1. What Linkway does

Linkway is a canvassing and volunteer coordination tool for small political campaigns and nonprofits. Campaign organizers create a Linkway account and configure their campaign. Volunteers use a shared link to log canvass contacts and voter interactions from their phones or computers. All canvass data is stored in a Google Sheet that belongs to the organizer's own Google Drive.

2. Information we collect

2a. Account and campaign setup

When you create a Linkway campaign, we collect and store in our campaign registry:

  • Your email address (via Google Sign-In)
  • Your organization or campaign name
  • Your chosen campaign slug (a short identifier used in your campaign's URL, e.g. smith2026)
  • Campaign type (e.g. electoral, ballot measure, nonprofit)
  • The file ID of the Google Sheet created in your Drive during setup

We use this information to route your volunteers' canvass submissions to the correct spreadsheet and to send you a welcome email with your campaign's volunteer sign-in link.

2b. Google Drive and Sheets access (organizer)

During campaign setup, your browser requests two Google permissions on your behalf:

  • Google Sheets (spreadsheets scope): used to create a new, blank campaign spreadsheet directly in your Google Drive. This action is performed entirely within your browser using your own Google identity — Linkway's servers never see your Drive credentials and do not perform this step on your behalf.
  • Google Drive file access (drive.file scope): used only to share the spreadsheet just created with Linkway's service account (a read/write bot), so that canvass data submitted by volunteers can be written to your Sheet. This scope can only interact with the specific file created during your setup session — it cannot access any other files in your Drive.

Once setup is complete, your campaign spreadsheet belongs entirely to your Google account. You can view, share, export, or delete it at any time from Google Drive without involving Linkway.

2c. Volunteer sign-in

When a volunteer signs in with Google to use the canvassing tool, we verify their email address against the Approved Volunteers list stored in your campaign's Google Sheet. We do not store volunteer email addresses on our own servers — the approved list lives entirely in your Google Sheet, which you control.

2d. Canvass activity

All canvass logs — doors knocked, voter contacts, support ratings, notes — are written directly to your campaign's Google Sheet via our service account. Linkway does not retain a separate copy of this data on our servers. Your campaign spreadsheet is the single source of truth, and it lives in your Google Drive.

3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and identify your campaign in our registry
  • Route your volunteers' canvass submissions to your campaign's Google Sheet
  • Send you a one-time welcome email from our Gmail account containing your campaign's volunteer link, a link to your spreadsheet, and getting-started instructions
  • Respond to support requests if you contact us

We do not use your information for advertising, sell it to third parties, or share it with anyone except as described in this policy.

4. Data storage and security

Linkway maintains a campaign registry (a Google Sheet controlled by Linkway) that stores, for each campaign: the campaign slug, the organizer's email address, the Google Sheet file ID, the campaign type, and an active/inactive status. This registry is used solely to route incoming requests to the correct customer spreadsheet. Linkway does not store organization names, volunteer lists, or canvass records in this registry.

All canvass data, volunteer lists, campaign configuration, and organization details live in your own Google Drive account, protected by Google's infrastructure. Linkway accesses your Sheet only through a service account (linkway-bot@linkway-501103.iam.gserviceaccount.com) granted Editor access to your specific file during setup. The service account cannot access any other files in your Drive.

5. Google API services

Linkway uses Google APIs, including Google Sign-In for authentication and the Google Sheets and Drive APIs for campaign setup. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically:

  • We use the Google Sheets (spreadsheets) scope only to create the campaign spreadsheet in the organizer's Google Drive during setup. We do not read or modify any pre-existing Sheets.
  • We use the Google Drive file access (drive.file) scope only to share the newly created spreadsheet with our service account. We do not access any other files in your Drive.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to third parties except as necessary to provide the Linkway service (writing canvass data to your own Google Sheet via Google's APIs).
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising purposes, and we do not allow humans to read user data unless you have explicitly requested support from us and granted access, or it is required for security or legal compliance.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your campaign registry entry (slug, email address, Sheet file ID, campaign type, and status) is retained while your campaign is active. If you want your campaign removed from our registry, email us at the address in Section 9 and we will delete your entry. Your campaign spreadsheet in Google Drive is yours to delete at any time directly from Google Drive — no need to contact us.

7. Children's privacy

Linkway is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of Linkway after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy. For material changes to how we use data obtained through Google APIs, we will notify affected users by email.
